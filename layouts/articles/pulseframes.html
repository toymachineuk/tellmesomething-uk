WHERE IT STARTED

How We Came Up with the Idea

PulseFrames didn't begin as a product. It began with a simple question:

Why should great stories disappear after 24 hours, while traditional websites often struggle to capture attention?

That question led us to explore what happens when the immersive language of social storytelling is brought to the open web.

Born from our R&D phase in Finland and refined through work with Pori Jazz and Prizz Tech, PulseFrames bring together Nordic technical precision and British creative thinking.

WHY PULSEFRAMES

Your story. Your rules. No platform lock-in.

Social Stories are built for attention in the moment. PulseFrames are built to keep working afterwards.

A PulseFrame lives on your own domain, remains searchable, and can continue bringing people to your site long after it is published
