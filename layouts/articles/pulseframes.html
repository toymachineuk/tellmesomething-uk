How We Came Up with the Idea

PulseFrames didn't begin as a product. It began with a simple question:

Why should great stories disappear after 24 hours, while traditional websites often struggle to capture attention?

We wanted to create something that combined the immediacy and immersion of social stories with the permanence, searchability, and ownership of the open web.

Born from our R&D phase in Finland and refined through work with Pori Jazz and Prizz Tech, PulseFrames bring together Nordic technical precision and British creative thinking.
